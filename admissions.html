Admission process

Five steps from first inquiry to the first day. Edit them to match your school’s process.

  1. Send an inquiry

    Fill in the form below or contact the office so we know you are interested.

  2. Visit the school

    Meet our team, see the classrooms and ask your questions.

  3. Submit the application

    Complete the application and provide the required documents.

    A friendly conversation or short assessment, where applicable.

  5. Confirm admission

    Receive the decision and complete enrollment formalities.
